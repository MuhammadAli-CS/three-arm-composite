\section{The functions, written out}
\label{sec:functions}

Every map in the sweep is $g_s(z)=\prod_{i=1}^{m}(z-s\,a_i)$ with
$a=\mathrm{linspace}(-2,2,m)$. The code \texttt{spr<m>s<100s>} records the
degree $m$ and the spread $s$; at $s=0$ every root collapses to the origin and
the map is simply $z^m$. The last column is the measured average number of $h$
values consistent with one observation.

\noindent Bold $\bar n$ marks the two injective members, $z^3$ and $z^5$.
Even powers fold at the origin, so $z^4$ and $z^6$ are two-to-one and are not
injective however the roots are placed.
